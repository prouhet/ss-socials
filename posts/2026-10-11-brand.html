Caption (draft)

Getting down on the floor and back up again sounds simple until it isn't.

It takes leg strength, balance, hip mobility and a little confidence. Every one of those things is trainable at just about any age.

The floor is a great test of strength. If you can get down to play with a grandchild or pick something up off the floor, and get back up without using the couch, you have a freedom a lot of people lose.

Could you get up off the floor without using your hands? Be honest. 👇
