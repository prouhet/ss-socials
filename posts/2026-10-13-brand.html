Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: I'm 55 and haven't exercised in about ten years. Is it too late to start?

A: You're not as far behind as you think. We begin by finding out where you are today. We look at how you move and what hurts. Then we talk about what you can already do and what you want to get back to. Then we start small, with basic strength training and walking, and build at a pace your body can handle. Most people are surprised by how quickly things like stairs and getting up from a chair start to feel easier. If you have health concerns, it's smart to check with your doctor first. We'll work with whatever they recommend.
