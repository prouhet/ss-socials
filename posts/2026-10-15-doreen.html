Caption (draft)

When we say training is personalized, this is what we mean.

Say your knee acts up on deep squats. A template says do them anyway. A coach says let's find out why first. Maybe we change the depth, swap the movement, strengthen what's around it, and keep you moving forward without making it worse.

If you've been told to push through, or you've stopped training because of pain, we'd like to talk. Of course, if the pain is sharp or sudden, see a doctor first. Send us a message.
