Caption (draft)

Something hurts, so you stop training. Two weeks later you haven't started again.

There's a better move: modify, don't quit.

Change the range of motion, the weight, the exercise, the speed. You keep the habit alive while your body sorts itself out. That's the hard part to rebuild.

Of course, not all pain is the same, persistent or sharp pain deserves a doctor's look. For the everyday stuff, a good coach can almost always find a way to keep you going.

What's a movement you've quit because it hurts? Tell us. Maybe we can help. 👇
