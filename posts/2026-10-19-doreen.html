Caption (draft)

A few things worth knowing about strength training through menopause. 💛

1. Muscle and bone density both tend to decline faster during this transition, which is exactly why lifting matters more now than ever.
2. You don't need to go heavy on day one. Start where you are and build up.
3. Protein, sleep and recovery matter more than they used to.
4. Consistent beats intense. Two or three sessions a week that you'll actually stick with will outperform a perfect plan you drop.

Of course, none of this replaces talking to your doctor about what's right for your body. It should still help you feel less lost about where to start.
