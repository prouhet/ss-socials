Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: I'm afraid that if I lift weights I'll get bulky. Is that true?

A: Short answer: no. Building visible bulk takes years of specific, dedicated training, heavy eating, and for most people, a good dose of genetic luck. What most people notice from regular strength training is that they feel firmer, stronger and more capable. Their clothes fit differently too. For women especially, lifting is one of the best things you can do for your bones and your energy. You'll still look like you, just a stronger version.
