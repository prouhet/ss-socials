Guessing isn't a diet plan.

Most of us eat by feel, read a few labels and hope for the best. Then we wonder why our energy crashes or the scale won't move. The problem usually isn't willpower. It's that we're working off hunches about what food does in our own bodies.

Knowing what works for your body is what makes it something you can stick with, instead of another diet that fizzles out.
