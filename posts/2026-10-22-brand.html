Caption (draft)

“I need to get in shape before I come in.”

We hear this a lot, and we get it. But it's a bit like tidying up before the cleaning service arrives. That part's on us.

You don't have to prepare. You don't have to lose the weight first. You don't have to build the habit first. You don't have to fix the knee first. You have to show up as you are, and we'll start from there.

Because the people who wait for the perfect moment to begin usually end up waiting a long time.

What's the thing you think you need to handle first? Tell us below. 👇
