Caption (draft)

Before a young athlete lifts heavy, they should lift well.

Good form is the foundation. Learning to squat, hinge, push and pull with control teaches the body how to move safely, and it makes every added pound count later, done more effectively. Rush to heavier weights too soon, and bad habits, along with sore joints, are what you get.

The best strength programs for teens are patient ones. A good coach watches every rep.

Curious what that patient, coached approach looks like in practice, parents? Send us a message and we'll talk it through.
