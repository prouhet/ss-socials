Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: I'm 58. How long should I be able to hold a plank?

A: For most people over 40, 30 to 60 seconds with good form is a solid target, and a clean minute is strong. There's no trustworthy plank chart for people in their 50s (we looked), so ignore the viral ones. The moment your hips sag or your lower back takes over, the set is done, even if the timer isn't. Three clean 20-second holds beat one ugly two-minute one.
