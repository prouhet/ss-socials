Signed up for a Thanksgiving morning turkey trot? Two weeks is plenty of time to get ready. 🦃

Walk or walk-jog three times a week.
Make one of those outings a little longer than the rest.
Keep up your strength work twice a week. Strong hips and legs protect your knees.
If you need new shoes, get them now, not the night before.
On race day, start slower than you think you should.

And if you walk the whole thing? That counts. You still got up and did it while everyone else was basting.
