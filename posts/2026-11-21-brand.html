Holiday breaks are great for rest, but a few weeks off can undo a lot of good strength work for young athletes.

The fix is simple: two short strength sessions a week through the holidays, plus plenty of sleep. It keeps them ready for the second half of the season and helps protect knees and ankles when they're back on the court or the mat.
