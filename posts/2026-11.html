Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: I keep hearing about creatine for women over 40. Should I be taking it?

A: Creatine is one of the most studied supplements out there. For women in their 40s and beyond, it helps your muscles do a little more work in each session, and that adds up when you're strength training. November is National Diabetes Month, so here's one thing worth knowing: your muscles are one of the biggest places your body sends sugar after a meal.

The more muscle you have and the more you use it, the more room there is to put that sugar to work. That's one reason strength training a couple of times a week is recommended for people managing blood sugar, alongside regular walking.

You don't need a big program to start. Two short strength sessions a week and a walk after dinner is a solid place to begin. If you're managing diabetes or prediabetes, check with your doctor first and bring us their recommendations. We'll build around them.

Q: I'm 58. How long should I be able to hold a plank?

A: For most people over 40, 30 to 60 seconds with good form is a solid target, and a clean minute is strong. There's no trustworthy plank chart for people in their 50s (we looked), so ignore the viral ones. The moment your hips sag or your lower back takes over, the set is done, even if the timer isn't. Planksgiving tip from me: pick the plank level that lets you hold it well.

Wrists cranky? Drop to your forearms.
Full plank too much right now? Put your hands on a bench or the kitchen counter. The higher the surface, the easier it gets.
Back talking to you? Knees down, and keep a straight line from knees to shoulders.
