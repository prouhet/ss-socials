Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: I've got a cold. Should I still work out?

A: A lot of trainers use the "neck check." If it's all above the neck, like a stuffy nose or a mild sore throat, a lighter workout is usually fine. If it's below the neck, like a fever, chest congestion, body aches or an upset stomach, rest.

And if you might be contagious, stay home and let us move your session. Your training partners will thank you. When in doubt, check with your doctor.
