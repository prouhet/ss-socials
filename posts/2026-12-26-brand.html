Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: I got a vibration plate for Christmas. Can it replace my workouts?

A: Enjoy it, but no. Some studies suggest vibration training may help a little with balance and leg strength in older adults, but the research is mixed and the effects are small. It doesn't load your muscles and bones the way lifting does.

Our take: use it as a warm-up or a little extra, and keep strength training as the main event. If you have a pacemaker or joint replacement, are pregnant, or have another health condition, check with your doctor before using one.
