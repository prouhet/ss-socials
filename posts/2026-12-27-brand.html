Caption (draft)

Four minutes a day. That's it.

Researchers at Penn State had adults 65 and older do four simple moves every day, 30 seconds each with 30 seconds of rest:

Push-ups (wall or counter is fine)
Chair stands
Band rows
Stair steps

Compared with a group that didn't exercise, they could do about 4 more chair stands in 30 seconds and held a one-leg balance about 3.6 seconds longer. Small daily habits add up.

Try it this week while the holidays scramble your schedule. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor first.
