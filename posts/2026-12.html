Ask the Trainer 💬

Q: Is it too cold to keep walking outside?

A: For most healthy adults, no. Cold-weather walks are great. A few things make them better:

Dress in layers you can unzip as you warm up.
Wear shoes with real tread, and slow down on anything wet or shiny.
Cover your ears and hands. They get cold first.
Warm up for a few minutes inside before you head out.

If you have a heart or lung condition, or the cold makes your chest tight, check with your doctor about your limits. And when it's icy, laps inside count too.

Got a question for us? Drop it below. 👇
